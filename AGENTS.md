# Multi-Agent Workflow Orchestration

GitLeadQualifier coordinates evaluation, safety verification, and compliance approval across specialized agent personas.

## Pipeline Architecture
1. **Step 1**: Ingest raw web form submissions and marketing lead payloads.
2. **Step 2**: Validate email domain authenticity and filter temporary disposable inboxes.
3. **Step 3**: Calculate composite ICP qualification score based on firmographic attributes.
4. **Step 4**: Assign and route qualified opportunities to account executives.

## Separation of Agents

Maker: GrowthMarketingEngineer who sets up inbound lead capture forms and enrichment APIs.

Checker: RevenueOperationsDirector who audits lead scoring models, territory splits, and conversion rates.
